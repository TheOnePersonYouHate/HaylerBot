## 11. CODE — Anti-repetition prompt rule  *(rebuild)*
- Symptom (from a live transcript): Bosun looped "Make it quick, I've got lines to
  tend." across replies. Responses were NOT broken — just repetitive.
- Root cause: **grok-4.3 rejects BOTH `frequency_penalty` and `presence_penalty`**
  (confirmed 400 errors), so the code's anti-loop penalties are unavailable on the
  xAI path; and the 120-msg history fed the model more of its own lines to echo.
- Fix: `brain.py` added a "VARY YOUR LANGUAGE" instruction — don't reuse
  catchphrases/sign-offs from your own recent chatter; advance the moment.
  Verified: 3/3 fresh replies, no loop. NOTE: prompt-level only (not a hard guarantee).

## 15. OPEN ITEMS / KNOWN ISSUES

1. **⚠️ CONTINUITY OF ACTIONS — STILL BROKEN (top priority).**
   NPCs don't reliably sustain an in-progress physical action/state across turns
   (e.g., after "knocks and waits" they may not keep behaving as if waiting at the
   door; ongoing actions get dropped or reset). Suspected contributors:
   - History stores only the spoken/followup lines; there is no explicit "current
     action / pending state" the NPC is instructed to hold and resolve.
   - Each NPC only receives its own `location`, not a running "what I'm currently
     doing" flag; the movement follow-up resolves after 15s but nothing tracks the
     *unresolved* wait.
   - Anti-repetition + register logic may push the model to "move on" instead of
     holding the beat.
   Candidate fixes to explore next session: add a per-NPC **pending-action/state**
   field (persisted per channel, injected into the prompt: "You are currently: …"),
   and/or a rule to continue an unresolved action until explicitly released. NOT
   YET IMPLEMENTED.
2. **Irinchev (XO NPC)** keeps `xo`/`exec`/`executive officer` aliases — collides
